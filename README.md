# Goaly Support & Community

Welcome to the Goaly support hub! This is your central place for getting help, reporting issues, and connecting with the Goaly community.

## About Goaly

Goaly is a smart scheduling assistant that helps you achieve your personal goals by automatically finding time in your busy calendar. Whether it's reading, exercising, or learning a new skill, Goaly seamlessly integrates with Google Calendar to schedule your goals at the most convenient times.

## Getting Support

### 💬 Join our Discord Community

The fastest way to get help, share tips, and connect with other Goaly users is through our Discord server. We are currently beta testing the 2.0 version of the app over there:

**[Join Goaly Discord](https://discord.gg/M9v98tjkn3)**

### 🐛 Report a Bug or Request a Feature

Found a bug or have an idea for a new feature? Please open an issue on GitHub:

**[Open an Issue](https://github.com/rosenpin/Goaly/issues)**

When reporting a bug, please include:
- Your OS (Android/iOS) and OS version (IE: iOS 26/Android 16)
- Steps to reproduce the issue
- Screenshots (if applicable)
- Any error messages you encountered

### 📧 Send Logs for Debugging

If you're experiencing technical issues, you can send us your app logs directly from the Goaly app:

1. Open Goaly
2. Tap your profile icon
3. Select **Report issue**
4. Choose **Send logs**

This helps us diagnose and fix problems faster!
